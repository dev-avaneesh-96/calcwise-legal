About Calcwise

Calcwise is a free, all-in-one collection of Indian finance calculators, built on a simple idea: every money decision should be easy to check before you make it.

THE IDEA

Financial products are usually explained with jargon, fine print and optimistic projections. Most people end up guessing — what a loan really costs, what a SIP actually grows to, which tax regime leaves more in hand, or how much a scheme like PPF or SSY pays at maturity. The math behind these answers is well known; it's just scattered and inconvenient. Calcwise puts 40 calculators — covering investments, savings schemes, loans, interest, retirement, salary, tax and trading — in one clean app, so the number you need is always a tap away.

THE PURPOSE

• Clarity before commitment — see a loan's true cost (EMI, total interest, month-by-month amortization, the effect of prepayments) and an investment's real growth (year-wise, adjusted for inflation if you want) before you sign anything.

• Compare, don't assume — put two loan offers side by side, check old vs new tax regime, flat vs reducing rate, or what stepping up a SIP each year changes.

• Private by design — every calculation runs on your device; we never ask you to sign in and never collect your financial inputs. The app shows ads (served by Google AdMob) and is free because of them. Your income, loans and investments are nobody's business but yours.

• Works everywhere — no internet needed. Every calculator works offline, in ₹ with lakhs and crores, using current Indian rules (FY 2025-26 tax slabs, capital gains rates, scheme limits).

Calcwise is a planning and estimation tool, not financial advice — always verify final figures with your bank, fund house or a qualified professional before acting.
